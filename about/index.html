About Nexora Media

Nexora Media is an independent media technology service. We help creators and media businesses turn video they already have the rights to into short-form content for social platforms.

What we are

Nexora Media provides editorial and technical services for turning authorised long-form video — podcasts, interviews, livestreams, and event recordings — into short-form clips suitable for platforms such as TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. We work with individual creators as well as media businesses that hold or license the rights to their own content.

Our approach

Every engagement starts with confirming the requester's right to have the content reformatted — either because they own it, or because they hold an explicit licence or written authorisation from the rights holder. Only once that is established do we review the source footage, identify the moments worth clipping, and produce short-form edits.

Who we work with

  • Independent creators repurposing their own podcasts, streams or videos.
  • Media businesses and publishers with an existing, rights-cleared content library.
  • Event organisers and brands with recorded footage they are authorised to redistribute.
