Content & Rights Policy

Nexora Media only works with video that the requester owns, licenses, or is otherwise explicitly authorised to have reformatted and redistributed. This page explains how that is verified and enforced.

Last updated: 2026

1. Authorisation is required before any work begins

Before we review, edit, or publish any video, the person or organisation requesting the work must confirm one of the following:

  • They are the original creator or copyright owner of the source video.
  • They hold an explicit licence from the rights holder that permits reformatting and redistribution as short-form content.
  • They have written permission from the rights holder covering this specific use.

We record which of these applies for every project. If none can be confirmed, we do not proceed.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not clip or repurpose video sourced from the open internet without the requester's own rights to it.
  • We do not redistribute content beyond the scope of the authorisation we were given.
  • We do not remove or alter copyright, ownership, or attribution information embedded in source material.

3. Publishing on a creator's or business's behalf

Where a customer connects their own social media accounts (for example, via TikTok's official developer platform) so that Nexora Media can publish approved clips for them, we act strictly within the permissions they grant through that platform's own authorisation flow, and only publish content that customer has reviewed and approved.

4. Copyright complaints and takedown requests

If you believe content produced or published through Nexora Media infringes your copyright or other rights, contact us at the address on our Contact page with:

  • A description of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed.
  • The specific content and location (URL or platform) you are reporting.
  • Your contact details and a statement of your relationship to the rights holder.

We will investigate every report in good faith and remove or disable access to content where infringement is confirmed.

5. Disputes between customers and third parties

If a dispute arises over whether a customer genuinely held the rights they represented to us, we will cooperate with the customer and, where legally appropriate, with the rights holder to resolve it, including removing disputed content while the matter is investigated.
