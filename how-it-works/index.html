How it works

From authorised source video to a published short-form clip

Our process is the same for every project: confirm the right to use the content, review it, cut it, and prepare it for publishing.

1

Rights check

We confirm you own the source video or hold explicit licence/permission to have it reformatted and redistributed. No footage is worked on before this is established.

2

Source review

We review the full-length video — a podcast episode, stream recording, interview or event footage — to find the moments most likely to work as standalone short-form clips.

3

Editing & formatting

Selected moments are cut, reframed for vertical/short-form video, captioned where useful, and prepared as a set of individual clips.

4

Review & delivery

Finished clips are reviewed against the original authorisation before delivery, so nothing goes further than what was actually agreed.

5

Publishing

Where a creator or business has connected their own social accounts, approved clips can be scheduled or published to platforms such as TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels on their behalf.

6

Ongoing rights record

We keep a record of the authorisation each project was based on, so there is always a clear answer to "who gave permission for this."

What we will not do

We do not source video from the open internet on a creator's or business's behalf without their own rights to it, and we do not clip or redistribute content where authorisation cannot be confirmed. See our Content & Rights Policy for the full detail on how we handle rights, takedown requests, and disputes.
