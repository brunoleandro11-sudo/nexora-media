Licensed content, reformatted for social

Nexora Media turns authorised long-form video into short-form content people actually watch.

We help creators and media businesses take video they already have the rights to — podcasts, streams, interviews, event footage — and turn it into short-form clips for platforms like TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. We never source, use, or distribute video without the rights holder's authorisation.

What we do

A rights-first approach to short-form content

Nexora Media exists for creators and media businesses who already own, license, or have explicit permission to repurpose their video content. We do the editorial and technical work of finding the moments worth clipping, cutting them to a short-form format, and preparing them for publishing — the rights work happens first, always.

Podcasts & interviews

Long-form conversations become a set of short, self-contained highlight clips.

Livestreams & events

Recorded streams and event footage are reviewed and cut into shareable moments.

Brand & media libraries

Media businesses with an existing content library get help repackaging it for short-form audiences.

Why it matters

Authorisation comes before editing

Before any video is touched, we confirm that the person or organisation requesting the work either owns the content outright or holds an explicit licence or written permission from the rights holder to reformat and redistribute it. That check is not a formality — it is the first step in every engagement, and it is documented. See our Content & Rights Policy for the full detail.
