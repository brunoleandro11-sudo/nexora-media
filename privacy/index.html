Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Nexora Media collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 2026

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details you provide (name, email address) when you reach out to us or request our services.
  • Source video and related materials you submit for editing.
  • Any information included in messages you send us.

Information from connected platforms

If you connect a social media account (such as TikTok) to our service to authorise publishing on your behalf, that platform will share with us only the information and permissions covered by the authorisation you grant during that platform's own OAuth login flow — for example, basic account identifiers and the specific publishing permissions you approve. We do not receive your platform password, and we only request the permissions our service actually needs.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our site may log basic technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited) for security and operational purposes.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the editing and publishing services you request.
  • To communicate with you about your projects or enquiries.
  • To verify content rights and authorisation as described in our Content & Rights Policy.
  • To maintain the security and proper functioning of our services.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. How we share information

We share information only:

  • With service providers who help us operate our website and editing workflow, under obligations to protect it;
  • With the specific social media platform you have authorised us to publish to, and only the content and actions you have approved;
  • Where required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Nexora Media, our customers, or others.

4. Data retention

We retain source video, edited clips, and related project information for as long as needed to provide our services and maintain the rights-authorisation record described in our Content & Rights Policy, or as required by law. You can request deletion of your information as described below.

5. Your rights and choices

  • You can disconnect any connected social media account at any time, through that platform's own settings or by contacting us.
  • You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • You can request that we correct or delete your personal information, subject to any legal or contractual obligations that require us to retain it.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via our Contact page.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for use by adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests relating to your personal information, can be sent to nexoramedia.contact@gmail.com or via our Contact page.
